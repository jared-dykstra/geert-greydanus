ENGLISH: Form under art. 2 of the First Implementing Decree, Public Servants' Legal Restoration Act 1946. The undersigned Greydanus, Geert, born Cubaard 1 Dec 1899, living Rotterdam, West-Varkenoordseweg 359b, occupation police brigadier, declares: I. that on 9 Apr 1943 at Rotterdam, in his post as police major, (a) he was dismissed with effect from 9 Apr 194[3] by the Chief Commissioner of Police because on the evening of that day he did not report for duty to take part in raids on Jews, but instead went into hiding, though he was arrested on 20-4-44. That in connection with this he did not perform his duty from 9-4-43 to 12-5-45 because of measures or the threat of measures by the occupier, and was paid no salary for 9-4-43 to 12-5-45. III. Places stayed: 1. 9-4-43 to 12-4-43 Rotterdam, Sichemstraat 14, c/o J. Berlijn; 2. 12-4-43 to end Apr 43 with H. Wiggers, farm Plak-an, Winterswijk; 3. end Apr 43 to 20-4-44 (interrupted for some weeks with family and acquaintances in Friesland) with H. Satter, now living C 99 Varsseveld; 4. 20-4-44 to 7-5-45 prisoner in Vught-Crefeld-Buchenwald.

Greydanus Geert; geboren te Cubaard op den 1 December 1899; wonende te Rotterdam aan de West-Varkenoordscheweg 359b; van beroep brigadier van politie.

II. [blank; f struck]
III. 1. van 9-4-43 tot 12-4-43 te Rotterdam aan de Sichemstraat 14 p/a of ten huize van J. Berlijn
2./3. van 12-4-43 tot einde April '44 bij H. Wiggers, hoeve Plak-an te Winterswijk.
4.-6. van einde April '43 tot 20 April '44, onderbroken door eenige weken verblijf bij familie en kennissen in Friesland, bij H. Satter, thans won. C 99 te Varsseveld.

Opmerkingen bij punt XII (first two paragraphs struck through diagonally - a draft):
[struck] "Thans, twee jaar na de bevrijding is betrokkene niet meer geinteresseerd en zeker niet meer in de stemming, in dezen iets meer te verzoeken dan toekenning der schadeloosstelling. Dit laatste, noodgedwongen.

[kept] "Op Punt XII wenscht betrokkene niet in te gaan. Omdat hij meent, recht te hebben op volkomen rechtsherstel, zonder dat hiertoe eerst een verzoek zijnerzijds wordt ingediend."
ENGLISH (struck draft): Now, two years after liberation, he is no longer interested and certainly not in the mood to ask for anything more than compensation, and that only out of necessity. Since the authorities, two years after liberation, have still offered no way to be rehabilitated quickly and fully, he no longer needs it. He asks for compensation out of necessity. (Kept): He declines point XII, believing he is entitled to full legal restoration without first applying.
