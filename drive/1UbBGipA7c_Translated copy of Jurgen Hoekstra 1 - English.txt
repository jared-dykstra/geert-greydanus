Who was Geert Greydanus?
Geert Greydanus was a detective at Justice in Rotterdam. He was one of the 600 employees here. Geert was like all employees hiding resistance fighters, but also detect and arrest Jews. Geert this work could not reconcile with his conscience and faith, so he decided in 1943 to go into hiding.
And even more, he was an active member of the resistance. 
Geert came from Friesland, was a Frisian at heart. He was married to Johanna Pebesma, daughter of Jan and Pebesma Wietske Baron. Wietske was the eldest sister of Keith Baron. When Jan could find no work in Friesland as farmhand family was moving to Limburg where John found work in the mines. But the band continued with Friesland (even up to the present day).
One of the children, a daughter, married Geert Greydanus, the detective. Together they moved to Rotterdam. The band with Friesland was strong, so Geert and Johanna were common in Suawoude.

And how about Geert?
He was active in the resistance. Wandered throughout the Netherlands. And also often came in Suawoude. Obviously the family. 
The Germans took his wife Johanna with the children as hostages caught in Vught. But Geert had Johanna agreed that in that case he would not give to the Germans. His wife and children would they release them, but if they got hold of him, it was unlikely to survive.
At some point in the east of the country, he was betrayed along with the group. He was transferred to Vught, where he was tortured. 
What the Germans did mostly with former policemen did not happen to him. He was sent to Buchenwald.
The Americans have taken this camp just before the Germans would begin to catch their "death march". And so Geert also survived Buchenwald.
He went on their own back to Rotterdam, to report back to the police.
And so all four main characters survived the war.
