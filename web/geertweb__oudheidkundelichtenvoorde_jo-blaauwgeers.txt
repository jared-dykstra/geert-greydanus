Jo Blaauwgeers
Jo Blaauwgeers was born on June 22, 1918, in Coevorden. After his military service, he joined the Royal Marechaussee. On December 24, 1942, while stationed in Groenlo, he married teacher Jo Dute from Betrum and went to live with her in rented rooms above Café De Boer in Harreveld. He was an informant for the Aalten underground resistance group, which was involved in stealing food ration coupons for people in hiding. On April 20, 1944, he was arrested and, via the Sicherheitsdienst in Arnhem, ended up in Camp Vught. He was sentenced to death and executed by firing squad in the dunes of Overveen on June 6, 1944. He was 26 years old. The Blaauwgeersstraat in Harreveld was named after him.
For more information, see the book Bezetting en Verzet, Part II, page 18.
