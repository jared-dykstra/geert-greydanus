Willem Doppenplein. Council Decision of 7 May 1992. [...]
Who was Willem Doppen? He was a quiet man who worked for the postal service as a mail carrier.
In addition, he ran a grocery store and drugstore (De Gaper) with his wife.
As a mail carrier, he combined his daily work with resistance activities, namely collecting addresses for people in hiding.
Later, he also distributed ration cards to those in hiding.
Doppen was arrested on April 20, 1944, due to the betrayal of an infiltrator.
He was transferred via Avegoor to the bunker in Vught, where the interrogations took place.
Shortly thereafter, he was brought before the judge, who sentenced him to work for Philips in Vught.
From there, he was transferred via Oraniënburg to Buchenwald, where he succumbed to the hardships on March 8, 1945.
